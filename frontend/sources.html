Map Guide & Data Directory

Learn how to read the dashboard measurements, active fire boundaries, and safety alert corridors, or explore the underlying telemetry feeds.

Wildfires & Heat Points

Wildfire Pins (Flame Badges)

Points representing official incident reports. Orange flame badges show active wildfires, and larger red flame badges show major fires exceeding 1,000 acres. Click a pin to view weather telemetry and containment levels.

Burn Perimeters (Charred Ground)

The dark charcoal/blackened boundaries mapped on the floor. Everything inside these shapes represents land that has already burned. These polygons expand over time as the fire progresses.

Active Heat (Satellite Hotspots)

Small glowing dots (yellow, orange, or red) representing thermal heat anomalies captured by satellites in the last 24 hours. Clustered dots on the edge of perimeters show exactly where the fire is currently active and spreading.

Smoke & Air Quality

Smoke Plume Shading

Shaded cloud layers showing smoke drift. **Light (yellow)** represents thin haze, **Medium (orange)** represents thick smoke in the air column, and **Heavy (red)** represents dense ground-level smoke.

Air Quality Index (AQI)

Dotted EPA monitoring stations across Utah. A **Green** dot indicates safe air. **Orange/Red** dots mean soot/fine particulate counts (PM2.5) are high—asthma sufferers and sensitive groups should limit outdoor time.

Understanding Weather Measurements

Wind Speed & Heading

Wind dictates the speed and path of fire expansion. High wind speeds feed oxygen to flames and loft burning embers downwind to spark new fires. **Wind direction** is written as the heading *where the wind comes from* (e.g., a "South Wind" blows *towards* the North).

Relative Humidity (RH)

Measures the amount of water vapor in the air. When **humidity is low (under 20%)**, the dry air rapidly sucks moisture out of forest trees, grass, and brush, turning them into highly flammable tinder.

Temperature

Higher temperatures accelerate fuel drying speeds. Combined with low humidity, high temperatures create optimal conditions for rapid fire spread and intense heat output.

Safety Corridors & Warnings

The tracker calculates **Wind-Driven Risk Corridors** (blue overlay cones extending from active fires). These show the path downwind along which smoke and fire embers are currently blowing.

When you type an address in the search box:

  • Critical Threat: The location falls inside an official active evacuation zone or municipality alert.
  • Warning Corridor: The location is downwind of a fire and inside the wind-driven risk cone, posing potential ember-lofting risks.
  • Safe: The location is outside active threat and wind-corridor lines.

This dashboard aggregates data across federal, state, and global spatial monitoring networks to deliver real-time wildfire intelligence. Below are details of every queried service, fields, and API endpoints.

Wildfire Incidents

NIFC WFIGS Point Locations

Retrieves geographical coordinates, incident names, discovery start times, containment percentages, cause determinations, and estimates of sizes (acres) for all active wildfires in Utah.

Provider: National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC)
Format: ArcGIS FeatureServer (REST API)
Sync Rate: 15-Minute Cache Protection
Meteorology

NWS Grid Observation Network

Polls current meteorological parameters (wind speed, wind direction, temperature, relative humidity) from weather sensors. Employs a fallback loop up to 3 stations if observation data is null.

Provider: National Weather Service (NWS)
Format: api.weather.gov (REST API)
Sync Rate: On-Demand per Incident Selection
Public Safety Alerts

FEMA IPAWS CAP Warnings

Queries live public safety polygons under Common Alerting Protocol (CAP), focusing on immediate evacuation mandates, threat alerts, or wildfire warning zones inside Utah.

Provider: Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)
Format: ArcGIS FeatureServer (REST API)
Sync Rate: Queried dynamically on page load
Atmospheric Quality

NOAA Satellite Smoke Plumes

Downloads daily vector outlines of smoke tracks detected by environmental satellites, categorizing them into Light, Medium, and Heavy densities representing aerosol scattering layers.

Provider: NOAA Satellite Analysis Branch
Format: ArcGIS FeatureServer (REST API)
Sync Rate: 15-Minute Cache Protection
Air Quality

OpenAQ / EPA AirNow Monitor Feed

Pulls hourly PM2.5 concentrations (µg/m³) from active particulate monitors across Utah. Mapped instantly to the official US EPA Air Quality Index (AQI) scale.

Provider: EPA AirNow Network / OpenAQ
Format: ArcGIS FeatureServer (REST API)
Sync Rate: 15-Minute Cache Protection
Satellite Heat

NOAA Satellite Hotspots

Queries real-time thermal anomaly pixels from MODIS and VIIRS satellite sensors. Features include Fire Radiative Power (FRP, measured in Megawatts), indicating combustion intensity.

Provider: NOAA Hazard Mapping System
Format: ArcGIS FeatureServer (REST API)
Sync Rate: 15-Minute Cache Protection
